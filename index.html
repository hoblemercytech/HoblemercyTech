Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Before Starting Your Project?

Find answers about our services, pricing, delivery process, payments, revisions and how HoblemercyTech can support your business.

What services does HoblemercyTech offer?

We offer website and app development, online store solutions, AI automation, AI content creation, branding, graphic design, business growth support, Snap & Edit photography and engineering solutions.

I am not sure what my business needs. Can you advise me?

Yes. You can book a consultation or complete our business assessment. We will review your current business setup, identify areas that need improvement and recommend the best place to start based on your goals and budget.

Can I order only one service without choosing a full package?

Yes. Every service can be ordered separately. The Launch, Growth and Elite packages are for businesses that want several related services together at a better combined value.

How much does a website cost?

Website pricing depends on the type of website, number of pages, required features, design complexity and integrations. After discussing your project, we will provide a clear quotation before work begins.

How long does a website project take?

A simple business or portfolio website may take about one to three weeks. Online stores, dashboards and custom applications may take longer depending on their features. You will receive an estimated delivery timeline before the project starts.

Can you redesign or improve my existing website?

Yes. We can improve your website’s design, mobile responsiveness, speed, content structure, user experience and functionality. We can also add features such as payments, booking, customer dashboards and automation.

What is AI automation, and how can it help my business?

AI automation uses smart systems to handle repetitive business tasks. It can answer customer questions, collect enquiries, send confirmations, follow up with leads, manage bookings, generate invoices and connect different business tools.

Can you build an AI chatbot for WhatsApp or my website?

Yes. We can create AI assistants for websites and supported messaging platforms. The assistant can answer common questions, recommend products or services, collect customer information and guide customers through booking or ordering.

Can you create or improve my online store?

Yes. We create product catalogue websites, one-page stores, complete e-commerce websites and multi-vendor platforms. We can also add payment gateways, delivery tracking, inventory systems, customer accounts, coupons and WhatsApp ordering.

What is included in a complete brand identity?

A complete brand identity may include your logo, brand colours, fonts, visual style, logo variations, business card, letterhead, invoice design, social media templates and basic brand usage guidelines. The exact items depend on the selected package.

Do you design flyers, promotional graphics and social media posts?

Yes. We design premium flyers, posters, promotional graphics, event designs, sales advertisements, carousel posts, social media covers and other marketing materials for different businesses and organisations.

What type of AI content can you create?

We create AI-generated images, character designs, promotional videos, faceless content, animations, movie posters, short films, brand story videos, product showcases and complete AI-assisted storytelling projects.

Can I book a Snap & Edit photo session?

Yes. Send your preferred date, location, type of session and the number of edited pictures you need. We offer portrait sessions, graduation photography, creative edits, beauty retouching and same-day delivery options where available.

Do you offer engineering and building design services?

Yes. Our engineering services include architectural and structural drawings, AutoCAD and Revit drafting, project estimation, foundation planning, setting out, site supervision and engineering consultation.

What information do you need before starting my project?

We normally ask for your name, business name, the service you need, your project goals, preferred style, deadline, budget range and any existing content or brand materials. The exact requirements depend on the type of project.

Do you offer payment in instalments?

Instalment payment may be available for larger projects. The payment structure will be agreed before work begins. Smaller design services may require full payment before production starts.

How many revisions do I receive?

The number of revisions depends on the selected service or project agreement. Your quotation will clearly state the number of included revisions. Additional changes outside the agreed scope may attract an extra fee.

Will I own my website, designs and project files?

After the agreed payment has been completed, the final project files and approved deliverables will be transferred to you as stated in the project agreement. Third-party tools, licensed assets, hosting services and external platforms remain subject to their own terms.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes. Support is provided based on the service and project agreement. Website maintenance, content updates, technical support and ongoing improvement plans are also available as separate services.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. We work remotely with clients in Nigeria and other countries. Communication can take place through WhatsApp, email or online meetings, while payments and delivery methods are agreed before the project starts.

How do I start a project with HoblemercyTech?

Contact us through the project form or WhatsApp and briefly explain what you need. We will review your request, ask any necessary questions, recommend the right solution and send you a quotation with the project scope, payment terms and estimated timeline.

Still have a question? Tell us what you are trying to achieve, and we will guide you toward the most suitable solution. Ask on WhatsApp
